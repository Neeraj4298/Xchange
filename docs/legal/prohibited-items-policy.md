# Xchange — Prohibited & Restricted Items Policy (v0.0 Draft)

**Effective Date:** November 2026  
**Document ID:** P0-T6-PROHIBITED

To protect community safety and comply with Indian law, the following items are strictly **prohibited** or **restricted** from being listed or requested in Wants on Xchange (`FR-LST-4`):

## 1. Strictly Prohibited Items (Zero Tolerance)
1. **Weapons, Firearms, Ammunition & Explosives:** Guns, knives designed as weapons, tasers, pepper spray, fireworks, and hazardous materials.
2. **Narcotics, Drugs, Alcohol & Tobacco:** Controlled substances, recreational drugs, e-cigarettes/vapes, liquor, and tobacco products.
3. **Prescription Medicines & Medical Devices:** Regulated pharmaceuticals, prescription-only drugs, and used medical consumables.
4. **Counterfeit, Replica & Stolen Goods:** Pirated media, first-copy/replica luxury goods, or any item you do not lawfully own.
5. **Adult & Explicit Material:** Pornographic content, adult toys, or explicit services.
6. **Live Animals & Wildlife Products:** Pets, livestock, ivory, or protected flora/fauna.
7. **Financial Instruments & Currency:** Cash-only listings, gift cards, crypto, securities, or government ID documents.

## 2. Restricted Categories (Disabled at v0.x–v1.0 Launch)
- **Motor Vehicles & Two-Wheelers (RTO-registered):** Restricted until title-transfer verification workflows are reviewed by legal counsel (non-motorised bicycles are permitted and encouraged!).
- **Real Estate & Property Rentals:** Out of scope.
- **Pure Services Barter:** Out of scope for v1.x (physical goods only).

## 3. Automated & Moderator Enforcement
- Every listing and want block is screened at creation time against our keyword and category blocklist (`packages/shared`) and image moderation pipeline.
- Violations result in immediate listing rejection or takedown and follow our three-step Strike System: **Warn → Suspend → Permanent Ban**.
