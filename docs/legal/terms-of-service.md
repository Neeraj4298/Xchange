# Xchange — Terms of Service (v0.0 Draft)

**Effective Date:** November 2026  
**Jurisdiction:** Bengaluru, Karnataka, India  
**Document ID:** P0-T6-TERMS

## 1. Eligibility & Account Rules
- You must be **18 years of age or older** to register and trade on Xchange.
- One account per individual verified via Indian (+91) Phone OTP.
- New accounts are subject to trust-ladder limits: maximum **3 active listings** and **2 simultaneous accepted trades** until your first completed exchange.

## 2. Nature of the Marketplace (No Money Custody)
- Xchange is a technology venue that discovers 2-way and 3-way barter matches between individuals.
- **Core exchange is free forever.**
- **No Custody of Funds:** Optional cash adjustments (`cash_paise`) are recorded strictly as peer-to-peer agreement metadata. Xchange does not hold funds, operate a wallet, issue stored-value credits, or provide escrow services. Any agreed cash adjustment is settled directly between participants at the physical meetup.
- **AI Estimates are Informational Ranges Only:** Any AI-generated item valuation displays a range and confidence band for convenience and is **not** a certified market price or appraisal.

## 3. Consent Before Lock & 3-Way Chain Rules
- An item is locked **only** after all participants in a direct offer or 3-way chain explicitly accept.
- In a 3-way chain (`A → B → C → A`), all three participants must accept within **24 hours**. If any participant declines or times out, the chain dissolves automatically with **zero penalty** and all items unlock immediately.

## 4. Safe Meetups & Handover Confirmation
- All exchanges in v1.0 occur via local in-person meetups at recommended public Safe Points (metro stations, malls, police-station-adjacent points).
- Each participant verifies the exchange in person using their **6-digit Handover Code**.
- Any trade problem or dispute must be reported within **48 hours** of completion (`FR-TRD-4`).
